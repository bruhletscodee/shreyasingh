# SpiceJet Crisis Response Playbook (Prototype)

## [PLAY-01] Protocol for Flight Delays
topics: Flight Delay
For flight delays extending beyond 60 minutes or cascading across multiple sectors:
1. Empathize immediately with passengers whose schedules and connections are impacted.
2. Acknowledge that airport ground personnel are working closely with flight operations.
3. Guide passengers to live airport display boards and [OFFICIAL_SUPPORT_CHANNEL] for confirmed gate and schedule updates.
4. Internal action: Notify Airport Operations to ensure airport gate agents are briefed and refreshments or meal vouchers are distributed in accordance with passenger charter policies.
5. Escalation: Route persistent or hostile complaints to Airport Operations.

## [PLAY-02] Protocol for Flight Cancellations
topics: Flight Cancellation
For last-minute or weather/technical flight cancellations:
1. Acknowledge customer distress and prioritize immediate assistance for stranded travellers.
2. Clearly explain available self-service rebooking and alternate travel options available via the website or airport service desks.
3. Advise customers on refund submission procedures without guaranteeing automatic fee waivers or immediate credits in public.
4. Internal action: Alert Airport Operations and Customer Support teams to coordinate rebooking queues and terminal assistance.
5. Escalation: Escalate group bookings or stranded high-risk passengers to Airport Operations and Customer Support.

## [PLAY-03] Protocol for Baggage Irregularities
topics: Baggage Issue
For delayed, misplaced, or damaged luggage:
1. Reassure the passenger that tracking and reuniting luggage with its owner is an operational priority.
2. Inquire whether a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) was filed at the destination airport baggage service desk.
3. Direct passengers to submit their PIR file reference number via DM or through [OFFICIAL_SUPPORT_CHANNEL].
4. Internal action: Forward luggage tag numbers to the Baggage Services team at the destination station.
5. Escalation: Route repeated mishandled baggage claims to Airport Operations.

## [PLAY-04] Protocol for Refund Inquiries and Payment Issues
topics: Refund
For delayed refunds, duplicate payment debits, or cancellation deductions:
1. Acknowledge the passenger's concern and explain the typical banking and verification cycle transparently.
2. Do not promise an exact date or hour for fund delivery in public social replies.
3. Request the booking reference (PNR) and transaction ID via private direct message or secure web portal [OFFICIAL_SUPPORT_CHANNEL].
4. Internal action: Check transaction status in reservation accounting systems and submit to the Refunds Team.
5. Escalation: Direct unresolved or high-value cases to Refunds Team.

## [PLAY-05] Protocol for Booking and Check-in Difficulties
topics: Booking/Check-in
For website errors, web check-in failures, seat allocation, and boarding pass retrieval:
1. Validate technical glitches promptly and suggest alternative steps (clearing cache, mobile app check-in, or airport counter check-in).
2. Clarify that airport counter check-in remains accessible if web systems encounter temporary maintenance.
3. Internal action: Report recurring digital errors to IT Support and Customer Support desks.
4. Escalation: Route booking payment drop-offs to Customer Support.

## [PLAY-06] Protocol for Customer Service Complaints and Staff Conduct
topics: Customer Service
For unhelpful staff complaints, communication blackouts, or lack of responsiveness:
1. Express genuine regret for the perceived breakdown in service quality.
2. Reiterate the airline's commitment to courteous, professional customer care.
3. Request trip date, flight number, and station details via DM so the station manager can investigate staff conduct.
4. Internal action: Log incident report with the Station Airport Manager for staff coaching.
5. Escalation: Escalate severe misconduct allegations to Customer Support management.

## [PLAY-07] Severity Ladder and Notification Matrix
topics: Flight Delay, Flight Cancellation, Customer Service, General Enquiry
The crisis framework categorizes social alerts into four severity tiers:
- **Critical (Score >= 8 or Safety Override)**: Immediate potential for viral brand damage, widespread operational shutdown, or safety concerns. Action: Alert within 15 minutes. Notify Head of Communications, VP of Airport Operations, and Duty Legal Counsel.
- **High (Score 6-7)**: Rapidly spreading complaints with high engagement, significant passenger friction, or regional disruptions. Action: Alert within 30 minutes. Notify Corporate Communications and relevant operational team lead.
- **Medium (Score 3-5)**: Routine complaints requiring human review, isolated baggage or refund grievances. Action: Queue for standard agent review within 2 hours.
- **Low (Score 0-2)**: Informational inquiries, compliments, or minor comments. Action: Monitor only, respond on demand.

## [PLAY-08] Safety and Security Protocol
topics: Customer Service, Flight Delay, General Enquiry
Any mention involving physical injury, medical crises on board or at the terminal, smoke, fire, engine malfunctions, security breaches, harassment, discrimination, or unsafe flight operations triggers this protocol:
1. Immediately bypass standard triage and assign **Critical** risk level.
2. Never comment on technical flight safety or accident causes in public.
3. Direct the user to emergency services or station medical authorities if an active emergency is ongoing.
4. Internal action: Immediate emergency dispatch to Safety & Security team and Corporate Communications.
5. Escalation: Mandatory escalation to `Safety & Security`.

## [PLAY-09] Viral Cluster and High-Spread Protocol
topics: Flight Delay, Flight Cancellation, Customer Service
When multiple distinct users post complaints about the same flight, airport station, or issue within a narrow window (theme growth ratio >= 1.5):
1. Detect cluster and group affected mentions under the Emerging Themes dashboard.
2. Formulate a unified, verified holding statement in coordination with Corporate Communications.
3. Avoid posting contradictory individualized replies that could be screenshot and compared publicly.
4. Internal action: Station briefing and high-alert notification to Corporate Communications.
5. Escalation: Route cluster oversight to `Corporate Communications`.

## [PLAY-10] Media and Influencer Inquiry Protocol
topics: General Enquiry, Customer Service
When an inquiry originates from an accredited journalist, news outlet, or verified creator:
1. Do not provide ad-hoc or personal responses.
2. Direct all media representatives to the official media desk at [OFFICIAL_SUPPORT_CHANNEL].
3. Internal action: Immediately forward post URL, reach, and summary to Corporate Communications PR team.
4. Escalation: Mandatory escalation to `Corporate Communications`.

## [PLAY-11] Emergency Holding Reply Template
topics: Flight Delay, Flight Cancellation, Baggage Issue, Refund, Booking/Check-in, Customer Service
When an automated draft fails validation, a rapid response is required during active operational crises, or human review is pending:

Holding reply:
"Thank you for reaching out. We understand your concern and sincerely regret any inconvenience. Our team is actively reviewing the situation. Please connect with our team at [OFFICIAL_SUPPORT_CHANNEL] so we can assist you directly."

## [PLAY-12] Post-Incident Audit and Operational Review
topics: Customer Service
Following the resolution of any High or Critical social crisis:
1. Preserve complete audit trail including original post, model spread probability, classifier labels, reviewer modifications, and logged escalation team.
2. Conduct retrospective with Corporate Communications and Airport Operations to assess response speed and message effectiveness.
3. Update triage rules, tone guidelines, and playbook chunks based on lessons learned.
