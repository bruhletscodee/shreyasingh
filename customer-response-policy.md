# SpiceJet Customer Response Policy (Prototype)

## [POL-01] Scope of Public Social Statements
Social media channels represent the public face of the airline. Staff and automated assistants may only state verified facts, general airline procedures, and established support workflows in public view. Case-specific decisions, passenger travel documents, and commercial settlements must never be negotiated or executed on public timelines.

Public statements may:
- Acknowledge service disruptions and apologize for distress.
- Direct passengers to designated self-service tools, check-in portals, and official help desks (`[OFFICIAL_SUPPORT_CHANNEL]`).
- Clarify general baggage allowance, web check-in windows, and standard boarding timelines.

Public statements may NEVER:
- State unverified departure or arrival times without live dispatch confirmation.
- Concede fault or legal liability for schedule disruptions or property damage.
- Provide definitive promises on refund approval, waiver of cancellation fees, or cash compensation.

## [POL-02] Verification Requirements for Authorised Disclosures
Any statement involving financial transfers, passenger re-accommodation, or flight operational status requires confirmation from an authorised internal source before communicating with the customer:
topics: Refund, Flight Cancellation, Flight Delay, Booking/Check-in

- **Flight Schedule & Cancellations**: Requires verification from Airport Operations Control Centre (AOCC) or station managers.
- **Refund Eligibility & Timelines**: Requires verification from the Refunds Team and passenger reservation records.
- **Compensation & Accommodation Claims**: Requires formal authorization in accordance with civil aviation requirements and airline tariffs.
- **Baggage Claims & Settlement**: Requires Property Irregularity Report (PIR) verification from Airport Baggage Services.

## [POL-03] Transition to Private Channels (Move to DM)
Customer interactions must immediately transition to private messaging (Direct Message) or secure customer support portals under the following conditions:
topics: Customer Service, Booking/Check-in, Refund, Baggage Issue

1. **Exchange of Personal Identifiable Information (PII)**: Any mention of PNR (Booking Reference), e-ticket numbers, passport details, phone numbers, email addresses, or payment card digits.
2. **Detailed Account Discussions**: Specific booking modifications, name corrections, itinerary changes, or refund status lookups.
3. **Severe Customer Grievances**: Escalated disputes, hostile interactions, or complex itineraries requiring step-by-step agent coordination.
4. **Platform Safety**: Public replies must never ask passengers to reply publicly with their PNR or contact details. Always instruct them to share via DM or [OFFICIAL_SUPPORT_CHANNEL].

## [POL-04] Approved Internal Escalation Pathways
When an issue exceeds front-line social media authority or poses operational or reputational risks, it must be routed to one of the approved escalation teams:

Approved Teams:
- **Customer Support**: For unresolved complaints, general booking assistance, web check-in failures, and standard customer follow-ups.
- **Airport Operations**: For active flight delays, gate confusion, baggage belt stoppages, ground crew coordination, and airport station updates.
- **Refunds Team**: For delayed refund processing, payment gateway debits, cancellation refund status, and fee dispute reviews.
- **Corporate Communications**: For media inquiries, viral social posts, high-follower grievances, journalist questions, and brand reputation risks.
- **Safety & Security**: For reported injuries, medical emergencies, unruly passenger incidents, security threats, aircraft safety allegations, or harassment.
- **Legal**: For formal legal notices, litigation threats, regulatory notices from aviation authorities, or allegations of statutory non-compliance.
